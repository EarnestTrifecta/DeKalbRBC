What We Believe

DeKalb RBC believes that doctrinal clarity is important for the unity of a congregation. Scripture is the highest authority and is the source of our beliefs. Christians have always expressed their beliefs from scripture in concise statements like creeds and confessions. A strong confession protects a church from error and serves as guardrails for the congregation.

Any who joins DeKalb RBC must affirm the 1853 New Hampshire Confession of Faith. Those members are responsible for believing and living in accordance with this confession. We believe the 1689 Baptist London Confession of Faith is the fullest expression of our faith. Our elders are required to subscribe the 1689 LCF. Additionally, we confirm the historic creeds of the Christian faith.

1853 New Hampshire Confession of Faith

I. The Scriptures

We believe that the Holy Bible was written by men divinely inspired, and is a perfect treasure of heavenly instruction; that it has God for its author, salvation for its end, and truth without any mixture of error for its matter; that it reveals the principles by which God will judge us; and therefore is, and shall remain to the end of the world, the true center of Christian union, and the supreme standard by which all human conduct, creeds, and opinions should be tried.
