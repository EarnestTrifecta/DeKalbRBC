Elders and Deacons

DeKalb RBC is a new church plant and is currently working to put in place elders and deacons to serve the church. Scripture teaches that a church should be led by a group of qualified elders while the deacons aid them in the service of the church.

Elders

Irby Wallace was raised in Fort Payne. He is a graduate of the University of Alabama in Civil Engineering and Beeson Divinity School with a Masters of Divinity. His wife, Jessica, and their three children, Dexter, Dottie Jo, and Doc, want to see Christians across DeKalb to grow in their faith and to see people saved.
